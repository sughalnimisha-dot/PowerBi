# PowerBi

1. Employee Quality Dashboard

Project Overview

The Employee Quality Dashboard was developed to analyze employee-related data and provide a consolidated view of workforce quality, performance, and organizational trends.

The objective was to transform employee-level data into meaningful KPIs and visualizations that help stakeholders quickly understand workforce composition and identify patterns across different employee segments.

Key Analysis

The dashboard enables analysis of:

Employee and workforce distribution

Employee quality and performance patterns

Comparison across different employee groups

Workforce trends and potential areas requiring management attention

Interactive filters allow users to move from an overall workforce view to specific employee segments for deeper analysis.

Business Value

The dashboard helps simplify workforce analysis and supports HR and management decision-making by converting employee data into easily understandable insights.

Skills: Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Data Modeling | HR Analytics | KPI Development | Data Visualization


2. Market Analysis Dashboard

Project Overview

The Market Analysis Dashboard was designed to analyze market-level data and identify performance patterns, trends, and potential business opportunities.

The dashboard converts complex market information into an interactive analytical view, allowing stakeholders to understand overall performance and investigate the factors contributing to different market outcomes.

Key Analysis

The dashboard supports:

Overall market performance analysis

Comparison of different market segments

Identification of high- and low-performing areas

Trend and pattern analysis

Identification of potential growth opportunities

The interactive structure allows users to move from overall market performance → segment analysis → opportunity identification.

Business Value

The dashboard provides a centralized view of market performance that can help businesses identify opportunities, understand underperforming areas, prioritize resources, and support data-driven market and growth strategies.

Skills: Power BI | Power Query | DAX | Market Analysis | Data Modeling | Segmentation | Trend Analysis | Business Intelligence
